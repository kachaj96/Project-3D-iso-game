# First sequence: the entrance to the crypt

Written by the developer on 2026-10-25 (translated from Polish). This is the first test of the graphics and of the story. Open points are marked as open on purpose; do not fill them in without asking.

## 1. Three maps

The sequence is three connected maps that build tension and lead to a meeting with a monster.

**Map 1, entrance to the crypt.** Based on the first art concept. The character reaches an old, dark crypt hidden in a forest. A stone gate leads into the tomb. This is the start of exploration and sets an uneasy mood. The player enters without knowing what waits inside.

**Map 2, main chamber of the crypt.** The inside of an old stone crypt. In the middle stands a coffin with the lid slightly open. Blood is on and around it and on the floor, suggesting that something terrible happened here. Blood traces lead towards stairs going down into the next part of the crypt. Two light sources:

- Natural light: tall, narrow stone windows in the north-west wall let in beams of cold light.
- Torch light: wall torches give warm orange light, their flames flicker gently and throw moving shadows on walls and floor.

The room feels abandoned, but suggests that something happened here recently.

**Map 3, depth of the crypt.** After the stairs the character reaches a more isolated room. At the back is a stone dais with an open book, the goal of the journey. A monster guards it, most likely the one that got out of the coffin in the previous chamber. It stands between the hero and the dais. This is where the introduction peaks and the first fight happens.

## 2. Story and course of the scene

**Stage 1, entering.** The hero comes to the crypt looking for a mysterious book. What it contains and why it matters is unknown for now and will be developed later. The hero crosses the entrance. Inside, the atmosphere becomes more oppressive. The hero hears faint whispers whose source cannot be identified.

**Stage 2, finding traces.** In the main chamber the hero notices the coffin with its lid open and the blood traces leading deeper. The player can tap the blood traces to show a short comment from the hero: "So it's true..." (Polish: "Czyli to prawda..."). It suggests the hero knew rumours or suspected something bad. No details yet. After examining the traces the hero goes to the stairs.

**Stage 3, meeting the monster.** Down the stairs the hero sees the monster standing in front of the dais with the open book. The hero freezes in terror. The monster is repulsive and threatening, and an obstacle: the hero cannot simply walk up and take the book. The hero decides to fight.

**Stage 4, defeat.** The fight begins. It quickly turns out the enemy is far stronger. The fight ends in the hero's unavoidable defeat. The hero falls, badly wounded, in a pool of blood, unable to go on.

**Stage 5, monologue and end.** The monster gives an unintelligible monologue, like a foreign language or words the hero cannot understand. It must build mystery, not explain events. After the speech the monster walks to the dais and takes the book. The picture slowly darkens until the screen goes fully black. The scene ends without explaining what happens to the hero or why the book mattered.

## 3. Goals of the sequence

- Build unease and dread step by step.
- Tell the story through surroundings, sounds and the hero's reactions, not long dialogue.
- Use the coffin, blood and traces as clues that lead the player on.
- Show the monster as a threat far beyond the hero's power.
- End on a mystery that makes the player want to learn the rest.

Open, to be developed later: the book, the hero's past, where the monster comes from, the meaning of its monologue.
